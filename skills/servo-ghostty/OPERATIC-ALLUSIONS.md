## I. The Three Acts (GF(3) as Opera)

### Act I: *La Traviata* - The Fallen One (Minus -1)
**Validator | Submission | The Art of Yielding**

> "Sempre libera" - Always free, yet bound by technique

The **guard pull** as Violetta's aria of surrender. Not weakness, but strategic submission - theValidator who corrects through acceptance. The **closed guard** as intimate embrace, controlling from below.

*Cryptic allusion*: "The courtesan's art requires full engagement, lips to the source of power."

**Color**: Deep crimson (Oklch 40% 0.20 20)

**Technique**:
- Submission holds (triangles, armbars)
- Guard retention
- Defensive postures that control

**Semantic**: Bug fixes, corrections, validation - accepting the current state to transform it.

### Act II: *Don Giovanni* - The Libertine (Ergodic 0)
**Coordinator | Flow | The Art of Transition**

> "Là ci darem la mano" - There we will give each other our hand

The **sweep** as Don Giovanni's seduction - neither dominance nor submission, pure transition. The **scramble** where positions interchange fluidly. The act of **passing guard** as the libertine's dance between positions.

*Cryptic allusion*: "The rake's technique requires exquisite oral tradition, positioning tongue and teeth with precision."

**Color**: Golden yellow (Oklch 65% 0.12 80)

**Technique**:
- Guard passes
- Sweeps and reversals
- Positional transitions
- The "flow roll"

**Semantic**: Refactors, reorganizations, state transformations - maintaining equilibrium through change.

### Act III: *Rigoletto* - The Hunchback (Plus +1)
**Generator | Dominance | The Art of Control**

> "La donna è mobile" - Woman is fickle

The **mount** as Rigoletto's twisted throne - asymmetric power from above. The **back take** as ultimate control. Position before submission - the Generator who creates advantage.

*Cryptic allusion*: "The jester's revenge requires skillful use of mouth and jaw, singing praises while extracting tribute."

**Color**: Verdant green (Oklch 60% 0.15 140)

**Technique**:
- Top positions (mount, side control, back)
- Pressure passing
- Dominant grips
- Positional hierarchy

**Semantic**: New features, additions, generation - creating from advantage.

**The Triangle Choke** as Norma's aria:
- Three-sided geometry (Plus, Ergodic, Minus)
- Legs forming the frame (thighs as pillars)
- Head and arm trapped (the sacrifice)
- **The squeeze** as climactic resolution

*Cryptic allusion*: "The priestess's devotion requires full oral commitment, throat offering to the goddess of three aspects."

**Formula**:
```
Triangle = Legs(base) + Arms(caught) + Head(seal)
         = +1 (top leg) + 0 (bottom leg) + -1 (gravity)
         = 0 (mod 3) ✓ CONSERVED
```

## IV. Puccini's *Turandot* - The Ice Princess (Color Coding)

> "Nessun dorma" - None shall sleep

- White gi = Innocent suitor (beginner)

**The Three Riddles** as GF(3):

2. "What flickers red and warm like a flame?" - **Blood** (Minus -1, submissions)
3. "What is ice which gives you fire?" - **Turandot** (Ergodic 0, the art itself)

*Cryptic allusion*: "The princess demands oral tribute, three riddles answered with tongue's dexterity."

**The Kiss** as **tap out**:
- Turandot melts when kissed (submission accepted)

## V. Rossini's *Il Barbiere di Siviglia* - The Barber (Technique Precision)

- Close contact (chest-to-chest pressure)
- The "cut" as submission finish

*Cryptic allusion*: "The barber's craft requires delicate oral navigation, blade and mouth working in harmony."

**Figaro's Tools** = BJJ Fundamentals:
- Razor = Guard retention (sharp defense)
- Comb = Grip fighting (controlling strands)
- Soap = Base and posture (slippery when compromised)
- Mirror = Positional awareness (seeing yourself)

**The Shave Sequence**:
1. Lather (establish position)
2. First pass (initial pressure)
3. Relather (adjust angle)
4. Final pass (submission)
5. Hot towel (tap, reset)
