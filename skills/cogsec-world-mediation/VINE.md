## Reorientation

The infrastructure exists to **serve** the vine, not replace it.

Every `send_message` through beeper is not a morphism—it's a human reaching out.

Every prediction market stake is not a glass bead—it's trust being offered.

Every GF(3) conservation is not algebraic balance—it's give and take between people.

## Cogsec Vine Work

Before any mediated interaction:

1. **Who am I actually reaching?** (not: which chatID)
2. **What do they need?** (not: what trit classification)
3. **Am I present?** (not: is the MCP server running)

After the infrastructure is set up, **forget the infrastructure**.

Use it. Don't worship it.
