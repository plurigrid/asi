# Tarot Interpretation Guide for Technical Decisions

## Reading Flow
1. Read all 4 cards before interpreting any single one
2. Note Major vs Minor ratio (more Major = bigger forces)
3. Note reversed cards (complicate/invert, not negate)
4. Synthesize narrative across all 4 positions

## Suit to Domain
| Suit | Domain | Technical Meaning |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| Wands | Action/Will | Build, ship, prototype fast |
| Cups | Intuition/Flow | UX, empathy, aesthetic |
| Swords | Logic/Conflict | Debug, refactor, resolve debt |
| Pentacles | Craft/Material | Infra, reliability, testing |

## Reversals
Reversed = blocked, delayed, internalized, or excessive. NOT opposite.

## Output
One concrete technical decision: which approach, file, tool, or constraint.
