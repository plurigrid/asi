# para-mensch-commons

A single entry point for the **universal Para-mensch atom** — the categorical-cybernetics learner block whose three closures are Play (forward), Witness (residual/conservation), Coplay (parameter feedback) — and the constellation of skills that instantiate, compose, or audit it.

The mensch (observer / world-bearer / interviewer / scheduler / experimenter) rides exclusively in the **parameter slot `P`**. Substrate runs through the **data slot `S → T`**. The audit lives at the **residual port `M`** with a GF(3) trit. Conservation = `∑ trit ≡ 0 (mod 3)`. Three closures, one fixed-point.

## The atom

```
                  ┌─────────────────┐
       S ──data──►│                 ├──data──► T          ▷ Play
       P ─param──►│  Para-mensch    ├──param──► P′        ◁ Coplay
       ε ─witness►│      atom       ├──resid──► M  ┐
                  └─────────────────┘              │      ◇ Witness
                                                   ▼
                                            trit ∈ GF(3)
```

Three diagrammatic rewrite operations are the **three traces** of this open block. `Para(Optic(𝓒))` provides the home; `contextads` (Capucci–Gavranović 2024) provide the 2-categorical composition; the `random_walk_verifier` style of `yb-translator` certifies hexagon coassociativity.
